Draft Room

The visual system for the fantasy draft GUI: a court-orange accent and marigold on concrete neutrals, with a warm charcoal dark mode that's never blue-tinted. IBM Plex Sans is for words and Lilex is for numbers. The accent always means you: your action, your selection, your players.

Rules

Blue is never the primary accent. Other cool colors are fine where they earn a place, and lavender is a ready alternative.

The accent means you.

Your actions, your selection, your players, our valuation. It's never decoration, and filter state never uses it.

Marigold means you changed it.

Only for values you've edited (Δ, Exp GP). Clearing the value clears the color.

Green and crimson mean better and worse.

Only for comparisons: Edge, ranks, rating deltas, injuries. Never for branding or for buttons, apart from destructive hover.

Words in Plex, numbers in Lilex.

Uppercase labels are Lilex at 11px with +0.08em tracking. Headings are Plex with tight tracking. Don't add a third family.

Dark mode is never blue.

Neutrals stay warm charcoal or neutral black. No navy or slate grounds, and no blue-tinted greys or shadows.

Borders before shadows.

Only the app frame and floating messages cast shadows. Panels, tiles and slots are separated with borders.

Faint is never essential.

--faint fails contrast for body text. Use it for placeholders, dividers and struck-through drafted players only.
